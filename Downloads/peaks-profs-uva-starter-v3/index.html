WELCOME

Welcome to Peaks & Professors.

Peaks & Professors at UVA brings students and faculty together through outdoor adventures across Charlottesville and the Blue Ridge.

Hosted through the University of Virginia's Galant Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Peaks & Professors is a student-led initiative designed to create meaningful connections between students and professors outside the traditional classroom.

Our adventures will bring together faculty from schools and departments across UVA for everything from local day hikes to larger outdoor excursions. The goal is simple: explore somewhere new, meet interesting people, and have the kinds of conversations that don't always happen during office hours.
