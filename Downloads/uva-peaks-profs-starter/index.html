Welcome to Peaks & Professors UVA

Trade office hours for trail hours.

We are building a student-run CIO that creates small-group hikes where UVA students can connect with professors beyond the classroom.

The goal is simple: make faculty relationships more human, make the outdoors more accessible, and make Charlottesville's trails part of the UVA experience.

Questions

Before you hit the trail

Who can join a trip?

Draft policy: current UVA students, with faculty participation coordinated by the organization. Update this language after confirming CIO and university requirements.

Do I need hiking experience?

No. Publish a clear distance, elevation, pace, and difficulty rating for every trip so students can choose an outing that fits them.

How do sign-ups work?

For launch, a simple form plus email confirmation is enough. Later, you can add capacity limits, waitlists, waivers, and automated trip emails.

How will transportation work?

Finalize transportation and driver policies with UVA/CIO guidance before trips go live, then describe the process clearly on each trip page.
